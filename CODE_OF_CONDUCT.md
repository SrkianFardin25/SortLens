# Code of conduct

SortLens follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:
- Be kind and patient. Many contributors are learning; help them.
- Disagree about code, never attack people.
- No harassment, insults, or sharing anyone's private information (this includes photos).
- Keep discussion on topic in issues and pull requests.

Problems can be reported privately to the maintainer at shaikhfardin206@gmail.com. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, close issues, or block anyone who breaks these rules.
